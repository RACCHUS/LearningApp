Studying outside LearningApp: privacy and terms

Covers the outside-study companion: your own courses, videos and books, adding a page from a link, the browser extension, the floating study window and the embedded YouTube player. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Privacy

Your outside resources

When you add a course, video or book you study elsewhere, LearningApp keeps its name, kind, optional link, cost, the outline you paste, which topics you tick and which parts you mark studied. Signed in, only you can read them, and they are deleted when you delete the resource or your account. As a guest they stay on your device. LearningApp never visits or fetches your links. What you mark studied never counts toward readiness: only LearningApp's own checks do.

Adding a page from a link, a bookmark or the extension

The page's address and title travel in the part of LearningApp's address after the "#", which browsers do not send to servers. Nothing reaches LearningApp's servers until you press Add. The browser extension reads the address and title of your current tab only when you click its button; see the extension's privacy page.

Your own AI chat

The "second opinion" prompt is text you copy into an AI service of your choice. LearningApp sends it nowhere; that service's own privacy policy applies to what you paste there.

Floating window and reminders

The small study window and its timer keep nothing. If your browser already allows LearningApp to show notifications, it shows one when your planned time is up. LearningApp never asks for that permission for this.

The embedded YouTube player

LearningApp uses YouTube API Services to show YouTube's embedded player when you choose to play a video inside the app. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play. LearningApp does not record what you watch, and playing a video never marks anything studied. When you press play, your browser loads the player from YouTube (www.youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube receives that request, which tells it this device played the video, may store and read information on your device such as cookies, and handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. LearningApp itself does not store, read or collect anything on your device or from YouTube for the player.

Recommended resources

The list of other ways to study a goal is built into the app, so viewing it sends nothing. Links to providers are not paid placements, and LearningApp is not affiliated with those providers.

Optional product analytics

If you opt in to product analytics in Settings, LearningApp may count, in groups of five or more people only, whether learners use the outside-study companion and whether they take a LearningApp check after marking something studied. It never records the names or links of what you study outside LearningApp. Opting out deletes what was recorded.

Terms

  • You may list courses, videos, books and websites you study outside LearningApp. They belong to their providers, under their own terms. LearningApp does not host them, is not affiliated with their providers, and is not responsible for their content or availability.
  • Marking an outside resource as studied is your own note. Only LearningApp's own checks count toward readiness.
  • Some study resources can be played inside LearningApp using YouTube's embedded player. By playing a video this way you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
